Specialized Divisions

Engines of Scientific Discovery

Six dedicated wings driving innovation, peer review, astronomical observation, and environmental research.

Robotics Division

Designs and programs autonomous robotic systems, microcontroller sensor arrays, and 3D-printed chassis for competitive and real-world applications.

Arduino Sensor Logic 3D Printing Control Systems

Astronomy & Space Division

Operates telescope observation sessions, builds radio telescope arrays for 21cm hydrogen line emission, and competes in astrophysics challenges.

Astrophysics Radio Astronomy Stellar Mapping RTL-SDR

Chemistry Lab Division

Conducts experiments in organic synthesis, electrochemistry, water purification reactors, and leads public Chemistry Open Day demonstrations.

Electrochemistry Organic Synthesis Titration Purification

Biology Field Division

Pioneers vertical hydroponic garden designs, ecological fieldwork, cell microscopy, and urban food sustainability research.

Hydroponics Microscopy Ecology Botany

Research & Publication

Produces the bimonthly MSC Peer-Reviewed Journal, trains members in academic paper writing, and manages scientific literature review.

Peer Review Journal Publishing Data Analysis Methodology

Editorial & Design Board

Crafts science communication graphics, manages website architecture, visual typography, and digital media reach for MSC events.

Visual Design Science Comms Layout Media
Flagship Projects

Built by Students, Powered by Science

Real-world prototypes and research initiatives designed and deployed by MSC members.

Robotics Division • Completed

Autonomous Environmental Patrol Robot

Self-navigating robot equipped with MQ-135 air quality, temperature, and soil pH sensors for autonomous environmental mapping in urban green spaces.

Arduino Mega MQ-135 Air Sensor 2.5-Hr Battery
Biology Division • Ongoing

Vertical Hydroponic Garden System

Automated soil-less recirculating hydroponic system with timed spectrum LED grow lights and automated pH balancing for high-density urban crops.

Recirculating Hydroponics pH Control LED Spectra
Astronomy Division • Phase 1

Radio Telescope Array (21cm Line)

Small-baseline radio interferometer array constructed using repurposed satellite dishes and RTL-SDR software receivers to map galactic hydrogen emissions.

RTL-SDR 21cm Hydrogen Raspberry Pi
Chemistry Division • Completed

Electrochemical Water Purification Device

Low-cost electrochemical reactor utilizing graphite electrolysis to precipitate heavy metals and neutralize pathogens without harsh chemical additives.

Electrolysis Graphite Electrodes Heavy Metal Removal
